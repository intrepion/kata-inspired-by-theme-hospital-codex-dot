# Oddwell Hospital specification

## Autonomous design interview
The user authorized recommended defaults, including test seams; questions from Grill with Docs are resolved here rather than reopening design approval.
1. What makes the game distinctive? A warm, illustrated municipal clinic with absurd ailments: Echo Hiccups, Paperfold Fever, and Moonwalk Malaise. All art is original CSS/SVG. No commercial assets or copied illness names.
2. What is the management tree? Build → access → hire → assign → diagnose → route → treat → earn → expand → accredit. Placement must leave every door and active corridor patient connected to reception. Two-by-two rooms have a door immediately below their left tile. This makes choke points visible and consequential without wall-drawing UI.
3. What is the scale? Three scenarios unlock one, two, then three ailments. Each starts fresh with a larger budget and arrival pressure. All use a 12×8 floor. Optional obstacles in later maps require route planning.
4. How is failure recoverable? No death; unhappy patients leave. Missing rooms, staff or funds get actionable alerts. Sell rooms for 70%, dismiss staff, use one $1,800 grant. Bankruptcy below -$500 or 12 walkouts fails, with retry. A 720-second deadline prevents endless unsuccessful scenarios.
5. What tests? Authorized public domain seams: build/sell, hire/assign/dismiss, fixed simulation step, scenario objective predicate, serialize/restore. Browser seam: ordinary buttons, tiles, dialogs, reload and navigation. No private implementation tests.
6. What simplifications? Deterministic arrivals/ailments, certain cures after treatment, no real medicine, no research/epidemics/deaths, fixed door geometry, uniform wages, no audio. Fewer systems permit a complete readable campaign.

## Rules and acceptance criteria
- Simulation runs at integer ticks, one tick per game second. Pause prevents all simulation; construction works while paused. Speeds 1/2/4 only change tick rate, never domain results. Dialogs suspend ticks.
- Reception is (0,4). Four-neighbor corridor pathfinding excludes room footprints/obstacles. Build preview and commit both validate geometry, cash, unlocks, no overlaps, every door reachable, all corridor patients reachable. A door cannot be covered by a new room. Invalid commands change nothing.
- Consultation costs $850, Resonance booth $1,100, Unfolding studio $1,250, Groove lab $1,400. Rooms occupy 2×2. Staff cost $250 to hire, then $7/tick clinicians or $5/tick technicians. Expert clinicians cost $400 and $10/tick, working 1.5× faster. No room gets more than one staff member; reassignment immediately frees the old room. Clinicians can work anywhere, technicians cannot diagnose. Staff cost wages even unassigned.
- Patient arrivals begin after 6 ticks, then every 11/9/7 ticks by scenario. Each patient starts at reception, walks one corridor tile/tick, queues for consultation, then the corresponding treatment, then exits. Least-loaded suitable staffed room wins. Queue FIFO. Consultation takes 5 service ticks, treatments 7/9/11; experts round up progress toward faster completion. Busy reassignment suspends service until restaffed; selling returns occupants to the former door to seek another room.
- Patience starts at 180/170/160 and decreases each tick. Patients walk out at zero. A cure happens exactly once, earns $850/$1,050/$1,300 and records happiness clamped to 45–100 according to time spent. Diagnosis earns $100. Initial cash $5,500/$7,500/$9,500. Upkeep $1 per room/tick. Wages and operating costs continue while active even without patients.
- Scenario thresholds (all inclusive): 8/14/20 cures, satisfaction 65/70/75, cash $1,000/$1,500/$2,000. Win stops ticks and enables next scenario; final win shows a campaign ending. Fail stops ticks and permits safe retry. Campaign has no carry-over money or staff.
- HUD shows cash, income/outgoings, satisfaction, cures, time, speed, objective progress. Room inspector exposes queues/service, assignment and selling. Staff roster shows eligibility/wages/reassignment. Patient ledger exposes ailment, state and patience. Help, grants, pause, save, validated reload, restart, retry, and next scenario are visible ordinary controls. Reset requires confirmation and cancellation preserves the state.
- Save version/schema validation rejects malformed, nonfinite, inconsistent references, impossible geometry, inaccessible doors, unavailable rooms, excessive counts, negative tick or unsupported scenario. Saves cannot inject markup. Reload preserves simulation state but starts paused. One active save slot; no silent autosave overwrite.
- Semantic buttons, visible focus, descriptive tile labels and status announcements; no dependence on color alone. Floor scrolls horizontally on narrow screens; other panels stack. Reduced-motion respected. No audio or paid services.
