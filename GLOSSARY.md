# Oddwell Hospital
A playful hospital management world, populated by fictional ailments and cures.

## Language
**Patient**: A visitor seeking diagnosis and a cure for one fictional ailment.
_Avoid_: Customer, unit

**Consultation**: The diagnosis that identifies which treatment a patient needs.
_Avoid_: General treatment

**Treatment room**: A space equipped to cure one particular ailment.
_Avoid_: Ward

**Door**: The single corridor entrance to a room.

**Clinician**: A staff member trained for consultations and treatments.

**Technician**: A staff member trained for treatment rooms but not consultations.

**Patience**: A patient's remaining willingness to stay at the hospital.

**Satisfaction**: The average happiness of cured patients in the current scenario.

**Grant**: A one-time emergency injection of cash, available per scenario.

**Scenario**: One hospital challenge, ending in accreditation or failure.

**Accreditation**: Completion of all cure, satisfaction, and cash objectives.
